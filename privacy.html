Privacy & browser-based exam monitoring

This page explains the kinds of information Pyvanta may process to provide learning, assessment, subscription, and support features. It is a plain-language product explanation, not legal advice. The exact behavior depends on the features enabled by your institution and the current application configuration.

In short

Pyvanta uses account and learning information to operate the platform. Some exam configurations use fullscreen and browser-tab visibility events. These browser controls have limits and do not prove that cheating is impossible.

1. Information used by the platform

  • Account information: details you provide when creating or using an account, such as email and student profile fields.
  • Learning and assessment records: answers, submissions, scores, progress, streaks, and related exam records generated while using the service.
  • Subscription and support information: plan status and payment references you submit for administrator verification, plus support requests you send.
  • Technical events: information required for the site to function and, when enabled for an exam, browser visibility or fullscreen-related events.

2. Why information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, display learning content, grade submissions, maintain progress, manage subscription access, support students, and administer configured assessments. Access should be limited to the people and services that need the information to perform those tasks.

3. Storage and service providers

Pyvanta's current project uses Firebase services for authentication and application data. Data handling also depends on the Firebase project configuration, security rules, and any services enabled by the site. Do not enter passwords, UPI PINs, OTPs, or other secrets into code demos, support messages, or exam answers.

4. Browser-based exam monitoring

  • An exam may request fullscreen mode so the assessment fills the browser window.
  • The application can use the browser Page Visibility API to detect when the exam page becomes hidden, for example when a student switches tabs or minimizes the window. Configured rules may count these events or trigger a warning or submission.
  • Browser-based signals can be inaccurate or incomplete and cannot guarantee exam integrity.
  • This explanation does not mean that Pyvanta records camera or microphone feeds. Do not assume those devices are used unless a specific exam explicitly requests permission and explains why.
  • Students should read the instructions shown before an exam and ask their institution if a permission or monitoring rule is unclear.

5. Access, retention, and deletion

Student records may be available to authorized administrators for learning, assessment, support, and subscription management. Records may remain available after a subscription expires so learning history and exam records are not lost. The precise retention period and deletion process must be confirmed with the institution or service administrator; this page does not promise automatic deletion after a particular period.

6. Keeping your account safe

  • Use a unique password and sign out on shared devices.
  • Do not share login credentials or payment authentication secrets.
  • Check the payee and amount in your UPI app before paying.
  • Only run code you understand in the free browser demo; it is a learning tool, not a hardened sandbox.

7. Questions or privacy requests

For help with an account, exam record, subscription, or privacy concern, use the Pyvanta Help & Support page. If support requires sign-in, use the support form available in your student area. We have not listed a separate email address because no verified public support email was configured in the supplied project.
